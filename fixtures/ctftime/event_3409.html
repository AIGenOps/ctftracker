SAS CTF 2026 Finals

Tue, 20 Oct. 2026, 03:00 UTC — Tue, 20 Oct. 2026, 14:00 UTC 

On-site

Indonesia, Bali

A SAS CTF event.

Format: Attack-Defense 

Official URL: https://ctf.thesascon.com/

Future weight: to be determined

Rating weight: 49.50 

Event organizers 
  • SAS CREW

SAS CTF is an international competition for cybersecurity experts, held as a part of the Security Analyst Summit conference. The competition consists of an online Jeopardy qualification stage and on-site Attack-Defense finals.

We are inviting Top 8 teams that completed the SAS CTF qualification round and 5 regional winners of Kaspersky{CTF} 2026 (https://lp.kaspersky.com/ctf/) to compete for a share of the $18.000 prize pot at SAS 2026 in Bali, Indonesia on October 19-22.

Prizes

- 1st place: $10000
- 2nd place: $5000
- 3rd place: $3000

Sign in and register a team to mark your interest.

This event participation is restricted to prequalified teams only!
